# Larkspur Analytics Onboarding Guide

Larkspur Analytics is a fictional company, invented for this repository's demo.

## Your first week

On day one you receive your laptop, your accounts, and a buddy from your own team. Your buddy meets you for 30 minutes every day during the first week. By Friday of week one you are expected to have merged one small change, such as a typo fix in the documentation, to prove your tools work end to end.

## Tools you will use

The team chat is Slack. Work items live in Linear. Code is hosted on GitHub, and documents live in Notion. The analytics warehouse is called Cedar, and you request access to it through the access portal, which usually approves within one working day.

## The first ninety days

At 30 days you and your manager agree three goals in writing. At 60 days there is a check-in with the head of your department. At 90 days you complete a short self-review, and the probation period ends if both sides agree it has gone well. The probation period is 3 months.

## Who to ask

For laptop problems, email it-help or visit the IT desk on the second floor. For payroll questions use the people-ops channel. For anything about security, use the security-desk channel.
