# Larkspur Analytics Security Policy

Larkspur Analytics is a fictional company, invented for this repository's demo.

## Lost or stolen devices

If a laptop or phone is lost or stolen, report it to the security desk within one hour of noticing. The security desk is reachable on the internal chat channel called security-desk and by phone on extension 4410. After the report, IT locks the device remotely and rotates the employee's credentials. Do not wait until the end of the working day to report a loss.

## Passwords and sign-in

Every account must use the company password manager. Passwords must be at least 16 characters, and employees may not reuse a password across systems. Multi-factor authentication is required on email, the code repository, and the cloud console. Hardware security keys are issued to anyone with production access.

## Handling customer data

Customer data may only be stored in the approved data warehouse, which is called Cedar. Exporting customer data to a personal drive or an email attachment is forbidden. Support staff may view customer records only when they have an open ticket for that customer, and every view is logged and reviewed monthly.

## Reporting an incident

Suspected phishing, a leaked secret or unusual account activity is an incident. Report it through the security-desk channel, and the on-call engineer acknowledges within 30 minutes during business hours. A written post-incident review is due within 5 working days of closing any incident that touched customer data.
