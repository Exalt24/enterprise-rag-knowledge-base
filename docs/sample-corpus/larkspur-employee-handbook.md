# Larkspur Analytics Employee Handbook

Larkspur Analytics is a fictional company, invented for this repository's demo. Nothing here describes a real organisation.

## Paid leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid vacation per calendar year. Unused vacation carries over, up to a maximum of 8 days, and anything beyond that expires on 31 March. Sick leave is separate and is 10 days per year. Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for either parent, and it can be taken in two blocks within the first 12 months after the child arrives.

## Working hours and remote work

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's own time zone. Outside core hours people set their own schedule. Employees may work remotely up to four days a week. The team office in Lisbon is open every weekday, and every employee is expected to be there on the first Tuesday of each month for the all-hands meeting.

## Expenses and equipment

Meals while travelling for work are reimbursed up to 45 euros per day. Any single expense above 200 euros needs written approval from a manager before it is incurred. Receipts must be uploaded to the expense tool within 30 days, and claims older than 30 days are not paid. Each employee gets a 1,200 euro home-office budget once every three years, which covers a desk, chair or monitor.

## Learning budget

Every employee has a learning budget of 1,500 euros per year for courses, books and conferences. Conference travel is paid from the same budget. A manager must approve a conference before tickets are bought.
