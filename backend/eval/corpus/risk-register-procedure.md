# Risk Register Procedure

*Synthetic example document, invented for this repository's evaluation. It describes no real organisation.*

## 1. Purpose and ownership

Each programme keeps one risk register. The Programme Risk Lead owns it. Every project inside the programme keeps a local register, and the Risk Lead merges the local registers into the programme register on the first working day of each month.

## 2. What every entry must hold

An entry records an identifier, a title, the cause, the risk event, the effect, one named owner, the current score, the chosen response, an action due date and a residual score. The owner is a role, never a team. The response is one of four: avoid, reduce, transfer or accept.

## 3. Scoring

Likelihood is scored from 1 to 5 and impact is scored from 1 to 5. The score is likelihood multiplied by impact. A score of 1 to 4 is low, 5 to 9 is medium, 10 to 16 is high and 20 to 25 is critical.

## 4. Review cadence

Critical risks are reviewed every week. High risks are reviewed every two weeks. Medium risks are reviewed every month and low risks every quarter. A risk that misses two review cycles in a row is flagged as stale and the Risk Lead reassigns it to a new owner.

## 5. Escalation

A risk with a score of 15 or more is escalated to the Programme Board within 2 working days. A critical risk is also reported to the Sponsor within 24 hours of being scored. A new risk must be logged within 3 working days of being identified, even if the score is still provisional.

## 6. Closure

A risk is closed only when the owner records the reason and the Risk Lead countersigns the entry. Closed risks stay visible in the register for 6 months so that the team can see what was retired and why.

## 7. Risk based contingency

The cost contingency for the programme is built from the register. For each open risk, multiply the probability by the cost impact if it happens, then add the results together. This sum is reviewed every quarter and compared with the contingency held in the cost plan. If the sum is higher than the contingency held, the Risk Lead raises it with the Programme Board at the next meeting.

## 8. Links to other controls

A change request that adds or alters a risk must update the register before the change is approved. Any risk that is accepted must have its acceptance recorded with the name of the role that accepted it.
