# Lessons Learned Note: Project Cedar

*Synthetic example document, invented for this repository's evaluation. Project Cedar is an invented refurbishment project and no real organisation is described.*

## 1. Context

Project Cedar was a refurbishment that ran for 26 months. This note records what the project team would change. It was written at close-out by the Project Manager and reviewed by the Programme Risk Lead.

## 2. Change requests came too late

Of the 31 change requests raised during the project, 14 were submitted after the work had already started. Two of them were later rejected, which meant the work had to be undone. The team recommends that no CR is accepted after the fact except for an emergency change.

## 3. The risk register went stale

At the 18 month review, 9 of the 40 open risks had not been reviewed for more than 3 months. Several of them described events that had already happened. The team now flags any risk that misses two cycles and reassigns its owner.

## 4. Forecasts were too optimistic

At the 50 percent complete point the forecast at completion was understated by 7 percent compared with the final outturn. The forecast had been rolled forward each month and not rebuilt. The team recommends an independent forecast review at 50 percent and at 75 percent complete.

## 5. Contingency ran out early

Contingency was drawn faster than the work was delivered: 62 percent of it was gone by the 40 percent complete point. The team recommends releasing contingency in 3 tranches, each tied to a stage gate, so that the last tranche cannot be spent early.

## 6. Handover took longer than planned

The asset register was updated only at the end, and handover took 6 weeks instead of the 2 weeks planned. The team recommends updating the asset register every month, as part of the cost report cycle.

## 7. Actions adopted

The Programme Board adopted four of the recommendations: no retrospective change requests, the stale risk flag, independent forecast reviews and tranche release of contingency. The monthly asset register update was adopted for new projects only.
